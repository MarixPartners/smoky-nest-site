Best 2-Bedroom Cabin Alternative in Pigeon Forge for Families and Couples

Many two-bedroom rentals in the Smokies are mountain cabins — often with a loft for the second bedroom, a shared bathroom, and a steep, winding drive up the ridge. The Smoky Nest is built for the four-person trip instead — two king suites, each with its own full bathroom, on easy flat roads about three minutes from the Pigeon Forge Parkway.

Why it works so well for four people

Two king suites, not a king and a bunk room

Both bedrooms have a king bed, so neither couple — or neither set of parents — draws the short straw. There's no loft to climb to and no “kids' room” with a queen squeezed in. A pull-out sofa in the living room sleeps one more if you're traveling as five.

A private bathroom for each bedroom

Each king suite has an attached full bathroom, so mornings before Dollywood don't turn into a line for the shower. (The second suite's bathroom can also be reached from the hallway, which makes it easy to share with a guest on the sofa bed.)

Flat-road access — no mountain driving

Many Smokies cabins sit up steep, narrow, switchback roads that can be stressful in a minivan, after dark, or in winter weather. The Smoky Nest is reached on easy, flat roads, with driveway parking for two cars — handy when two couples arrive in separate vehicles.

Close to everything, without being on the Parkway

You're about 3 minutes from the Parkway and a short drive from the attractions people come for — roughly 1.3 miles to the Smoky Mountain Alpine Coaster, about 1.8 miles to The Track, and about 5.7 miles to Dollywood — while the house itself sits in a quiet residential setting.

Room to slow down

Cook a real breakfast in the fully stocked kitchen (Keurig, air fryer, Instant Pot, blender), light the indoor fireplace on a cool evening, or take coffee to the screened back porch. A full-size washer and dryer, fast Wi-Fi, and Roku TVs cover the practical side of a longer stay.

Is it a good fit for you?

Great for: two couples traveling together, parents with two older kids or teens, grandparents bringing grandchildren, and couples who want a bigger, quieter home base than a hotel room.

Maybe not for: groups wanting a hot tub, game room, or a long-range mountain view from a deck — those are the trade-offs of a ridge-top cabin, and The Smoky Nest trades them for easy access and two true king suites.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many people does The Smoky Nest sleep?

Up to five: two king suites sleep two each, and a pull-out sofa in the living room sleeps one.

Does each bedroom have its own bathroom?

Yes. Both king suites have an attached full bathroom. The second suite's bathroom can also be reached from the hallway.

Are there steep or winding roads to get to the house?

No. The Smoky Nest has easy, flat-road access — no steep or winding mountain roads — and driveway parking for two vehicles.

How far is The Smoky Nest from Dollywood?

About 5.7 miles, and about 3 minutes from the Pigeon Forge Parkway. Drive times vary with traffic, especially on busy weekends and holidays.

Are there stairs inside?

The interior is all on one level. There are three steps up to the front door.
